## New in 2.0.1
A fix for 2.0.0. Everything else is as in [2.0.0](https://github.com/mugetsu-aqw/mugetsu/releases/tag/v2.0.0).

**Fixed**
- /spellcraft: on the "Pick a Spell Ink" screen every button read "Requires No Rank" instead of the ink it needs. This came with 2.0.0's colour-custom fix, which could put wrong text on other screens of the game in the same way. The colour-custom fix now only touches the players' gear; gear colours are still right from the first moment.

**Good to know**
- Still being looked at, from one report each: a target's HP and mana showing 0 before a fight, and screenshots or streams of Mugetsu showing a half-black picture. If you see either, please tell us where: the room and the target, or the program you capture with and your Frame pacing setting.
